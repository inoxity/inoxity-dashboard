Privacy Policy for Inoxity

Effective Date: February 2026

Inoxity is a research application designed to collect sleep-related data for academic research purposes.

Data Collected

  • Sleep data from Apple Health (with user permission)
  • User-provided SONA ID (not linked to user identity)
  • Optional survey responses
  • Optional uploaded screenshots/screen-recordings (e.g., Screen Time)

How Data Is Used

Data are used solely for academic research to study relationships between sleep and daily behaviors. Participation is voluntary. All studies are IRB approved prior to Inoxity use.

Data Storage

Data are securely transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in a secure research database (Supabase).

Data Sharing

Data are not sold or shared for marketing purposes. Data may be used in aggregate form in academic publications.

User Control

Participants may opt out at any time and request deletion of their stored study data within the app.
