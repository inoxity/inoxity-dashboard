# About the team

Inoxity was developed in the [Cognitive Communication Science Lab](https://cogcommscience.ucdavis.edu/people) (CCSL) at the University of California, Davis. It was created to support high-throughput data collection in naturalistic settings, facilitating research on sleep, media use, and dynamic behavioral processes in everyday life, and is shared openly so other research teams can run these studies without building their own infrastructure.
