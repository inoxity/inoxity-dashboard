# Managing a research team

The dashboard lets a study owner invite collaborators and assign study-specific roles.

## Roles

The study owner can invite collaborators as **Admin**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**. Use the lowest role appropriate for each collaborator's duties.

| Role | Dashboard permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | View, edit, activate, and archive the study; manage collaborators; delete the study; transfer ownership |
| Admin | View, edit, activate, and archive the study; manage collaborators; cannot delete the study or transfer ownership |
| Editor | View, edit, activate, and archive the study; cannot administer collaborators |
| Viewer | Read-only study access |

Any accepted collaborator may remove themselves from the study.

## Invite a collaborator

1. Open **Research Team**.
2. Select the study.
3. Enter the collaborator's email address and choose a role.
4. Send the invitation.
5. The recipient opens the invitation and signs in or creates the matching researcher account to accept it.

Pending invitations are associated with the invited email address. The dashboard shows pending invitations to the signed-in user whose email matches the invitation.

## Role and ownership changes

Authorized team members can change collaborator roles. The dashboard also includes a separate ownership-transfer workflow. Ownership transfer has broader consequences than a role change and should be reviewed before confirmation.

## Data access is separate

Dashboard collaborator roles do not grant access to the research team's Supabase project or participant data. The research team must separately govern who can access or administer the Study Backend and exported participant data.
