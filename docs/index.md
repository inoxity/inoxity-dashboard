## What is Inoxity?

Inoxity is a research platform with two participant and researcher touchpoints:

- The **Inoxity Dashboard** is where researchers create and manage studies.
- The **Inoxity iOS app** is where participants enter a study code, complete study-specific onboarding, and take part in configured activities.

Behind those interfaces, an Inoxity-operated **Control Backend** resolves study codes and returns routing information. Each study uses a separate **Study Backend** owned by its research team for participant records and collected data.

## Who is it for?

Inoxity is intended for research teams running appropriately reviewed studies with iPhone participants. It is especially suited to studies that need one or more of the following:

- configurable Apple Health data collection;
- scheduled or windowed external/in-app surveys;
- participant reminders;
- optional photo or video collection;
- study-specific onboarding, support information, FAQs, and completion messaging;
- separation of participant data into a study-owned backend.

Inoxity does not replace your research protocol, consent process, IRB or ethics review, data-governance plan, or institutional security review.

## What participants experience

A participant opens the iOS app and enters the code supplied by the research team. The app retrieves that study's current configuration, connects directly to its verified Study Backend, and presents only the onboarding steps and features the study enabled. Depending on the study, participants may enter an identifier, choose a start date, grant Apple Health or notification access, complete surveys, upload media, review local summaries in **See My Data**, or withdraw.

Participants do not create conventional email-and-password accounts in the app. Supabase anonymous authentication is used behind the scenes for participant operations. The Control Backend authentication service may create an anonymous technical account for study-code resolution, but it does not store that account as a study enrollment.

## Major components

| Component | Role | Holds participant study data? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Researcher Dashboard | Creates studies, stores versioned configuration, manages collaborators, and generates Study Backend setup SQL | No |
| Participant iOS app | Runs onboarding and configured study activities | Keeps local app state and pending sync data on the device |
| Control Backend | Resolves study codes and supplies configuration/routing metadata; authentication may create an anonymous technical account | No enrollment records, participant identifiers, or collected study data |
| Study Backend | Stores enrollment, survey events, configured HealthKit samples, media metadata/files, and withdrawal records | Yes; one separate Supabase project per study |
